## 1. Editorial & Strategic Overview

This breaking investigative analysis covers the controversial proposal to transform the shuttered 1.5 million-square-foot **San Francisco Centre** (former Westfield Mall and Bloomingdale's at 845 Market Street) into a hybrid AI data center, public AI exhibit, and technology living lab.

The article explores the intersection of:
1. **Frontier AI Infrastructure**: The pivot toward "Physical AI" and robotics requiring downtown footprints.
2. **Urban Adaptive Reuse**: Why deep-floor retail caverns suit heat-shielded compute clusters better than residential housing conversions.
3. **Environmental & Municipal Politics**: San Francisco Mayor Daniel Lurie's emergency 45-day moratorium on new data centers and midterm election energy/water debates.
4. **The Net-Positive Blueprint**: KG Growth Partners and B3 Investors' proposal dedicating waste heat and water recycling to rooftop agriculture for homeless shelter food security.
