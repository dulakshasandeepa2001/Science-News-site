Daily Science News was founded by Dulaksha Sandeepa to bridge the gap between complex research institutions, planetary telemetry, and science enthusiasts globally. Every published article undergoes rigorous verification against peer-reviewed academic literature.

Editorial Verification Standards

We enforce a strict multi-tier factual verification process:

  • Primary Literature: All reporting is anchored in peer-reviewed scientific studies published in journals such as Nature, Science, The Astrophysical Journal, Lancet, and validated preprints on arXiv.org.
  • Agency Telemetry: Mission updates are cross-referenced with raw telemetry and official mission logs directly from NASA, ESA, JAXA, and CERN.
  • Context & Limitations: We transparently detail study sample sizes, confidence intervals, and research limitations.

Corrections & Updates Policy

Accuracy is essential to scientific integrity. When factual errors or ambiguous statements are identified:

  • We immediately rectify the inaccurate text.
  • A transparent "Correction Note" is appended to the report detailing the modification and timestamp.
  • Readers can report discrepancies directly to our editorial desk at contact@sciencenewshub.click.

Responsible AI Transparency Disclosure

We utilize AI tools strictly as research assistants for syntax formatting, data synthesis, and literature scanning. 100% of articles are researched, fact-checked, and approved by human editorial oversight under Dulaksha Sandeepa.

Editorial Independence

Daily Science News operates completely independent of external corporate sponsors, pharmaceutical firms, or aerospace defense contractors. Advertising displayed via Google AdSense adheres strictly to program policies and does not influence our editorial analysis.
