China's Latest AR Helmet Providing 360-Degree Visual Range from Inside Combat Tanks

New augmented reality helmets developed by China allow tank crews to 'see through' their armor, providing enhanced situational awareness with 360-degree visibility and integrated targeting systems.

✍️ By: Military Technology Correspondent 📅 Published: September 7, 2025 ⏱️ Read Time: 4 min read

1.Revolutionary AR Integration

China has reportedly been developing advanced Augmented Reality (AR) helmets for the crews of its military armored vehicles. This system connects to cameras and sensors on battle tanks and infantry fighting vehicles, providing a 360-degree view through a transparent helmet display.

2.Enhanced Visibility Through Armor

These AR helmets enable crews to "see through" the armor that normally restricts visibility in heavily protected vehicles. According to reports, crews can continuously view real-time video feeds as well as critical data such as vehicle status, ammunition count, and target information.

3.Current Deployment

This system is already being seen on the ZTZ-201 battle tank and China's latest military support vehicles. It provides crews with the ability to maintain situational awareness during day or night operations in both open terrain and urban environments. What has become apparent is that this technology can be adapted across various platforms according to requirements.

4.Offensive Capabilities

Beyond visual enhancement, these helmets have the capability to be used as an offensive support system, allowing gunners to target weapons by turning their heads or directing their gaze. This is functionally similar to the helmet system installed on the US Army's Apache helicopter, where the 30mm chain gun follows the gunner's line of sight.

5.Strategic Implications

This represents a significant advancement in battlefield technology, potentially giving Chinese armored units enhanced tactical awareness and engagement capabilities compared to conventional systems that rely on limited periscopes and view ports.

6.Global Technology Race

Military analysts suggest that this development signals an acceleration in the integration of augmented reality systems into conventional warfare, potentially triggering similar developments in other major military powers seeking to maintain technological parity.
