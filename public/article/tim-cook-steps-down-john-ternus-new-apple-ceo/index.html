Tim Cook Steps Down After 15 Years: John Ternus Takes Over as Apple CEO to Lead the AI Era

In a historic leadership transition, Tim Cook has stepped down as Chief Executive Officer of Apple after 15 transformative years at the helm, officially handing executive leadership to long-time hardware engineering chief John Ternus.

✍️ By: Technology & Business Editorial Desk 📅 Published: September 1, 2026 ⏱️ Read Time: 5 min read

1.Historic Leadership Transition at Apple Park

In a historic leadership transition, Tim Cook has stepped down as Chief Executive Officer of Apple after 15 transformative years at the helm, officially handing executive leadership to long-time hardware engineering chief John Ternus.

The handover concludes one of the most lucrative corporate tenures in modern history. Under Cook’s leadership since August 2011, Apple's stock surged by over 2,000%, briefly touching an unprecedented $5 trillion market capitalization and expanding from a consumer hardware manufacturer into a sprawling global ecosystem of services, wearables, and enterprise technologies.

Cook transitions into the role of Executive Chairman of Apple’s Board of Directors, where he will continue overseeing long-term corporate governance, global government relations, and supply chain strategy.

2.The Tim Cook Legacy: From Steve Jobs' Successor to a $5 Trillion Empire

When Steve Jobs named Tim Cook CEO in August 2011, industry skeptics questioned whether an operations expert could preserve Apple’s innovative DNA. Over the following decade and a half, Cook answered those doubts by scaling Apple to historic operational heights:

• Ecosystem Expansion: Cook pioneered high-margin web and digital services, including Apple Pay, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple TV+, and Apple Card, transforming iPhone users into recurring-revenue subscribers.

• Wearables Category Dominance: Under his direct watch, Apple launched the Apple Watch and AirPods, creating standalone multibillion-dollar product divisions that dominate global market share.

• Geopolitical Statesmanship: Cook navigated international trade tensions between the United States and China, safeguarding Apple’s complex manufacturing network while orchestrating gradual production diversification into India and Vietnam.

3.Who Is John Ternus? Meet Apple’s New CEO

At 51, John Ternus brings deep engineering roots to the chief executive role. Having joined Apple’s design team in 2001, Ternus steadily climbed the ranks to become Senior Vice President of Hardware Engineering, reporting directly to Cook.

• Engineering Pedigree: Ternus holds a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering from the University of Pennsylvania. Prior to Apple, he worked at Virtual Research Systems.

• Product Oversight: Inside Apple Park, Ternus led the engineering teams behind every flagship product line, including the iPhone, iPad, Apple Silicon transition (M-series chips), and the latest Mac lineups.

• Durability & Carbon Reduction: Ternus spearheaded corporate initiatives to improve product repairability, structural durability, and carbon-neutral component manufacturing.

In a farewell staff memo, Cook praised his successor: "Few people understand what it takes to build products that change the world the way John does."

4.The Immediate Road Ahead: Foldable iPhones and the Generative AI Race

Ternus takes leadership during a critical strategic inflection point for Apple. While the company maintains dominance over premium smartphones, it faces stiff competition in artificial intelligence integration from Silicon Valley rivals.

The September 9 Keynote Debut: Ternus faces an immediate trial by fire. Just eight days into his tenure, Apple hosts its annual September keynote on September 9, where the company is widely expected to unveil its first-ever foldable iPhone. Ternus will stand on stage not just as the engineering brain behind the hardware, but as the new public face of Apple.

The AI & Hardware Convergence: Rather than selecting software leadership for the CEO seat, Apple’s board opted for Ternus to emphasize hardware-driven AI adoption. With on-device neural processing units (NPUs) and private cloud compute driving the next cycle of smartphone upgrades, Ternus is positioned to unify Apple's silicon, battery architecture, and hardware design around local generative AI capabilities.

5.Tim Cook’s New Role as Executive Chairman

Cook is not stepping away from Apple completely. In his new capacity as Executive Chairman of the Board, Cook retains a mandate focused on:

• Global Policy & Diplomacy: Handling diplomatic and regulatory discussions with the White House, European Union regulators, and Asian trade ministries.

• Supply Chain Continuity: Assisting Ternus with global manufacturing logistics while maintaining US domestic investment commitments.

• Executive Mentorship: Ensuring seamless corporate continuity during Ternus’s first multi-year strategic roadmap.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Why did Tim Cook step down as Apple CEO?

After a 15-year tenure that increased Apple’s market valuation to $5 trillion, Tim Cook transitioned to Executive Chairman of the Board to focus on policy, board governance, and global supply chain strategy.

Q2: Who is the new CEO of Apple?

John Ternus, Apple’s former Senior Vice President of Hardware Engineering, officially became Apple’s CEO on September 1, 2026.
